# Politics and Data Analytics — Undergraduate Work
This repository contains a collection of academic work, practical exercises and projects completed during my undergraduate degree in Politics and Data Analytics at the University of Manchester.

The repository serves as an ongoing record of my academic development, with a particular focus on quantitative methods, statistical analysis, data science and political research.

## Repository Structure
Work is organised into folders by module code, with individual folders used to separate practical exercises, assignments and projects where appropriate.

The repository includes work from modules covering areas such as:
Quantitative methods and statistics — statistical analysis and practical exercises.
Data science and modelling — working with data and applying analytical techniques.
Politics and political research — exploring political questions through academic research and empirical analysis.
Independent research — coursework and projects developed throughout my degree.

## Purpose
This repository provides a central location for organising and maintaining my university work, tracking my progress over time, and developing my skills in data analysis, research and reproducible workflows.

As I progress through my degree, the repository will continue to grow to include further coursework, practical exercises and independent projects.

## Notes
This repository is primarily intended as an archive of my academic work. Some materials may have been developed for teaching or assessment purposes and may require additional context to be understood independently.
